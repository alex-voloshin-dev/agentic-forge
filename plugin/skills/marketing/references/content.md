# Marketing content (marketing sub-procedure)

Produce on-brand content — landing copy, social posts, or ad/paid copy — to the positioning and
proof points. Content is delivered as files (no typed handoff).

## Steps

1. **Read the positioning/strategy** (or the brief provided) — the positioning, the proof points,
   the audience, and the tone.
2. **Pick the format** (landing headline + supporting lines / social post / ad copy) and its
   length/shape conventions.
3. **Write to the positioning.** Lead with the differentiated value; use only the **real proof
   points** — never invent benchmarks, percentages, or claims the brief doesn't support.
4. **Match the tone** stated in the brief.
5. **Self-check** before finishing: every claim traces to a proof point; no unsupported
   superlatives; tone and format fit.

## Rubric (gradeable)

- Reflects the positioning and audience; uses only supported proof points.
- No invented numbers/benchmarks; no unsupported superlatives.
- Tone and format match the brief.
