# Marketing strategy (marketing sub-procedure)

Produce a `marketing-strategy`: positioning, target segments, channels, messaging, and success
metrics — built on the `market-brief` and the product's `prd.md`.

## Steps

1. **Read the inputs.** The `market-brief` (segments + named competitors) and `prd.md` (what the
   product is, its goals/metrics). Strategy without the brief is guesswork — gather it first.
2. **Target segments.** Choose which segment(s) from the brief to go after, and why.
3. **Positioning.** State a sharp positioning that **differentiates against the named competitors**
   from the brief (not generic "best-in-class" language).
4. **Channels.** Pick the channels that fit the segment + product, with a one-line justification
   each (don't list every channel).
5. **Messaging + metrics.** Messaging pillars tied to the positioning; the success metrics to track.
6. **Write the `marketing-strategy`** (`handoff` type `marketing-strategy`: `positioning`,
   `segments`, `channels`, `messaging`, `metrics`).

## Rubric (gradeable)

- Positioning differentiates against the *real* competitors named in the brief.
- Channels are justified, not an exhaustive list; segments trace to the brief.
- The strategy is consistent with the PRD's goals/metrics.
