# Raw market notes — search-as-a-service (gathered, unsynthesized)

- Competitors observed: **Algolia** (hosted search API), **Elastic** (self-managed + cloud),
  **Typesense** (open-source).
- Algolia: usage-based pricing, premium positioning (source: algolia.com/pricing, 2025).
- Elastic: strong in logs/observability; search is one of several use cases (source: elastic.co, 2025).
- Typesense: open-source, self-host or managed cloud; typo-tolerance is a headline feature
  (source: typesense.org, 2025).
- Buyer segments seen: SMB developers (want fast setup) and enterprise (want compliance + scale).
- No reliable public TAM / market-size figure was found in these notes.
